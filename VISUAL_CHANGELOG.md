- Replaced the previous space/HUD-heavy sidebar branding with a minimalist Dune-inspired wordmark.

- Added `ARRAKIS OPERATIONS` micro-label and a restrained divider under the brand.
- Replaced main/sidebar logo assets with the new minimalist SpiceConex / Arrakis artwork.
