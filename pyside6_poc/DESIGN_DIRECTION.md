# SSM-SpiceConex — Dune visual direction

This POC intentionally moves away from a generic dark dashboard.

## Principles
- Basalt/obsidian surfaces rather than brown panels.
- Sand and spice are accent materials, not the dominant palette.
- Procedural dune/topography texture is subtle and continuous.
- Navigation is an operations instrument, not a web-dashboard sidebar.
- Fleet entries use hierarchy and status rather than a spreadsheet-like table.
- Borders are restrained; depth comes from tonal separation and small shadows.
- The existing Dune skin is atmospheric only and never competes with operational data.
